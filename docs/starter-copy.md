# Starter copy for a Network chapter

Patterns on the front page use the short lines below. Longer pages are built in the Site Editor from this guide. The voice follows okfn.org and speaks as a chapter. The Foundation’s tool catalogue, staff list, and PayPal donate button stay on okfn.org.

Navigation: Who we are `/about/`, What we do `/what-we-do/`, Network `/network/`, Get involved `/get-involved/`, Blog `/blog/`, Contact `/contact/`. Donate is a button, not a menu item.

## Home

Heading: Openness is our foundation.

Lead: We believe knowledge should be accessible to everyone. This chapter advances open content, data, and technology in its own country, together with the Open Knowledge Network.

Button: Who we are → `/about/`

How we work:

- We include people. Meetings, training, and advocacy. → `/get-involved/`
- We build local capacity. Research, policy, and practical help with open data. → `/contact/`
- We are part of a network. Groups in more than 40 countries share tools and experience. → `/network/`

Then latest posts, then four topic cards:

- Global challenges. Open information helps people understand shared problems and see what is working.
- Democratic accountability. People need access to information about how public institutions work.
- Research, science, and culture. Knowledge people can use, reuse, and share.
- Consumers and corporate accountability. Information about who controls organisations, products, and services.

Newsletter heading: Don’t miss a thing. Text: Stay on top of what is happening in the open movement. Set `okfn_chapter_newsletter_url` to the chapter’s own list. Do not use the Foundation’s Mailchimp form.

Closing: Let’s build a world where knowledge is accessible to everyone. Button: Donate → `/donate/`

## Who we are (`/about/`)

Heading: We are open advocates.

Open knowledge is content, information, or data that people are free to use, reuse, and share, without legal, technical, or social restrictions.

Mission: We work for a fair, sustainable, and open digital future, and we treat open knowledge as a design principle, not only as data.

Vision: Governments, institutions, and movements use openness so people can reach the information they need to face the problems in front of them.

How we work: include people; build skills; design technical and social infrastructure; work across places and disciplines; make work that others can copy and adapt; put people and the planet first.

Person cards start as placeholders. Replace them with current people.

Legal placeholder: “This chapter is an independent non-profit. Add its legal name, country, and registration number. The Open Knowledge Foundation is a separate organisation.” Link https://okfn.org/en/who-we-are/governance/ and label it as the Foundation.

## What we do (`/what-we-do/`)

Heading: Open knowledge, applied here.

Intro: This chapter sets its own priorities. Replace these cards with the projects you actually run.

Three project cards, shipped as examples. Delete any you do not replace.

Tools, links only, with one sentence: the Open Knowledge Foundation maintains these tools; this chapter uses and teaches them.

- CKAN, https://ckan.org/
- The Open Definition, https://opendefinition.org/
- Open Data Commons, https://opendatacommons.org/
- Data Package, https://datapackage.org/
- Open Data Editor, https://okfn.org/en/projects/open-data-editor/
- Frictionless Data, https://frictionlessdata.io/

Also link the Network project repository, https://network.okfn.org/project/

## Network (`/network/`)

Heading: We are a crowd.

The Open Knowledge Network connects people in more than 40 countries. Members are independent. They share expertise, tools, and materials, and they decide their own work.

- Project Repository, https://network.okfn.org/project/
- Global Directory, https://network.okfn.org/specialist/

Joining this chapter goes to `/contact/`. Starting in a country with no chapter goes to network@okfn.org and the Network Guidelines on okfn.org.

## Get involved (`/get-involved/`)

Heading: Everyone is welcome.

No special background is required. An interest in open knowledge is enough.

Three paths: come to a meetup, help with a project, write to the chapter about a local group.

Link the Foundation code of conduct, https://okfn.org/code-of-conduct/, and add a local reporting address.

## Contact (`/contact/`)

Heading: Get in touch.

Use the footer email and social links. Contact Form 7 is optional.

## Donate (`/donate/`)

Heading: Support a fair, sustainable, and open future.

Money given here funds this chapter. Add the local payment method. Do not embed the Foundation’s PayPal button.
